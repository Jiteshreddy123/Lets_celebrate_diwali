# DIWALI UNLIMITED 🪔
### *Light. Sparkle. Share.*

A production-quality, responsive, interactive digital Diwali celebration experience. Visitors can light traditional clay diyas, play with an authentic Indian handheld phuljadi (sparkler), launch fireworks into the night sky, dedicate lights to their loved ones, and share personalized greetings on WhatsApp and social media.

---

## ✨ Features

1. **Auspicious Hero Experience**:
   - Deep midnight navy and warm golden ambience with floating stardust particles.
   - Interactive clay diya with an auspicious ignition ritual and smooth entrance animations.
   - Primary CTA: *"ENTER THE CELEBRATION"*.

2. **Interactive Diya Lighting**:
   - Handcrafted Indian clay diya SVG with terracotta textures and gold engravings.
   - Tap/click to ignite flame with authentic SVG/CSS flickering flame physics and golden aura.
   - Auspicious **Pancha-Diya Thali** collection (*Ananda, Shanti, Samriddhi, Arogya, Prema*) with distinct flame variations and a *"Light All Diyas"* ceremonial celebration button.
   - Diya ignition sound synthesized via Web Audio API.

3. **Handheld Phuljadi (Sparkler)**:
   - Realistic Indian wire phuljadi with metallic handle, dark combustible composition coating, and glowing incandescent core tip.
   - Touch and mouse tracking with authentic outward spark ballistic trajectory, gravity, fading trails, and charcoal micro-branching pops.
   - Four festive palettes: *Classic Golden*, *Silver Diamond*, *Royal Kesariya*, *Festive Magic*.
   - Interactive *Spark Burst* action and organic crackle synthesizer.
   - Canvas-isolated touch action to prevent unintended page scroll on mobile while swiping.

4. **Fireworks Night Sky**:
   - Interactive night sky canvas. Tap or click anywhere to launch ascending rockets with glowing trails.
   - Five spectacular explosion patterns:
     - Golden Chrysanthemum
     - Multi-colored Peony
     - Glittering Willow
     - Starburst
     - Radiant Ring
   - *"GRAND DIWALI SHOW 🎆"* automated celebration barrage.
   - Reduced-motion accessibility detection.

5. **Personalized Diya Dedication**:
   - Dedicate a diya to someone special (*Family, Friend, Partner, Mentor, Loved One*).
   - Generates an illuminated digital greeting card with animated flame and personal blessings.
   - Instant WhatsApp sharing with pre-formatted emojis.
   - Generates and downloads high-resolution 1080x1350 PNG greeting cards via HTML5 Canvas.

6. **The Shared Diya Wall ("TOGETHER, WE LIGHT UP THE WORLD")**:
   - Responsive mosaic of illuminated lamps.
   - Transparently distinguishes locally saved dedications (*"📍 Your Dedication"*) from community demo lights (*"🌍 Community"*).
   - Interactive heart reactions with sound.
   - LocalStorage persistence across browser reloads.

7. **Share The Celebration**:
   - Prominent sharing hub with WhatsApp direct link, native Web Share API, clipboard copy, and personalized name customizer.

8. **Final Greeting**:
   - *"THIS DIWALI, BE SOMEONE'S LIGHT."*
   - Grand celebration trigger and smooth scroll back to top.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **React 18 + TypeScript**
- **Vite**
- **Tailwind CSS**
- **HTML5 Canvas** (Ballistic particles, sparklers, fireworks, PNG card generator)
- **Framer Motion**
- **Lucide React Icons**
- **Web Audio API Synthesizer** (Instant offline festive sound effects: diya ignition, sparkler crackle, fireworks boom, temple bell chimes)
- **Web Share API & LocalStorage**
